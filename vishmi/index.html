Our mission

A civilisation is held together not by its laws, but by the quiet courtesies of its citizens.

Vishmi exists to make those courtesies easier — through tools that gently remind, nudge and enable. We believe a queue respected, a lane honoured, or a voice lowered in a shared space is a form of seva: small service to everyone around us.

We’re a young studio building software for real streets, apartments, transport networks and neighbourhoods. No lectures. No gamified guilt. Just considered products that help people do the right thing a little more often.

What we hold dear

Four quiet ideas, carried from older India into newer tools.

Rather than invent values, we’ve borrowed from a grammar we already live by — and translated it into the way we design, build and ship.

A lamp lit

Duty is the oldest form of care.

I.

Duty, softly held

Following rules isn’t obedience — it’s a quiet agreement we keep with each other. Our products make that agreement visible and easy.

II.

Respect as design

Every interface we ship treats users — and the strangers they share the world with — as adults worthy of dignity.

III.

Service over spectacle

We are not interested in virality. We are interested in the one honk that didn’t happen, the one bin that got used.

Passed along

The bowl matters less than the hands.

IV.

Conscience, at scale

Technology can either numb judgement or sharpen it. Vishmi chooses the latter — gently, and on purpose.

How we work

Six principles we won’t trade, even on bad days.

  1. 01

    We design for the person at the back of the queue, not the front.

  2. 02

    We prefer a nudge to a notification.

  3. 03

    We build with municipalities, housing societies and schools — not around them.

  4. 04

    We are suspicious of streaks, leaderboards and shame.

  5. 05

    Privacy is a form of respect. We treat it like one.

  6. 06

    If a feature cannot be explained to a grandparent, we rewrite it.
